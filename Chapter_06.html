CHAPTER 06

HTML Multimedia

Allows us to add audio and video content to a webpage.

6.1 Audio

The HTML audio element is used to play sound files on a webpage. The controls attribute provides buttons such as play, pause, and volume.

♫

Listen to Audio

Audio Player

Replace the sample audio files with your own MP3 or OGG file.

Audio Controls

The controls attribute displays the browser's built-in audio player. Users can play or pause the audio, adjust the volume, and seek through the recording when supported.

Audio with Controls

The player appears even before the audio starts playing.

Loop Audio

The loop attribute makes the audio start again automatically after it reaches the end.

Repeat Audio

Play the audio to hear it repeat when it reaches the end.

6.2 Video

The HTML video element embeds a video file into a webpage. It can display playback controls, a preview image, and other video features.

▶

Watch a Video

Video Player

Replace the sample video with your own MP4 or WebM file.

Video Controls

The controls attribute displays buttons to play, pause, change the volume, and control playback. Available controls may vary by browser.

Video with Controls

Use the player controls to start or pause the video.

Video Poster

The poster attribute specifies an image that appears before the video starts playing.

Video Preview Image

Add a preview image named video-poster.jpg to your Images folder.

Muted Video

The muted attribute makes the video start without sound. Muted playback is often used for decorative or background videos.

Muted Video Player

This video is muted and set to repeat. Use the player controls to manage playback.

Autoplay

The autoplay attribute asks the browser to start playing media automatically. However, browsers commonly block autoplay when sound is enabled.

Muted Autoplay Video

Autoplay behavior depends on browser settings and policies. The video is muted to improve autoplay compatibility.

Chapter Summary

HTML multimedia makes webpages more engaging by adding audio and video. The audio element plays sound files, while the video element displays video content.

Important attributes include controls, loop, muted, autoplay, poster, and preload.
